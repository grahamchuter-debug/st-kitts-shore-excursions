Privacy

How this static guide treats information you share.

Contact form

The enquiry form on this site runs in your browser only. Submitting it does not transmit your details to our servers or send email automatically. If you email hello@stkittsshoreexcursion.com, your message is handled through ordinary email once that channel is active. We do not claim live routing from the website itself.

Hosting and logs

Like most websites, hosting infrastructure may record standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested. We use those logs only for security and reliability.

Cookies and analytics

This site is designed as a static guide. We do not require an account. If analytics tools are added later, this page will be updated to describe them.

Third parties

Tour operators and cruise lines have their own privacy practices. Booking with them is separate from this guide.

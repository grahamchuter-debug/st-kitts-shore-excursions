Methodology

How we treat ship schedules and excursion claims for St Kitts.

Ship schedule source

Port calls on the ship schedule are synced from the Caribbean Shore Excursions authority dataset for the St Kitts port slug only. Local QA checks totals by year and month against that source. We do not invent calls for other islands.

What schedules are for

Arrival and departure fields are planning guides. Cruise lines can change them. Always confirm with your ship before you book a shore activity.

Soft claims we avoid

  • Invented Scenic Railway departure times or guaranteed seat availability
  • Promising Nevis on every St Kitts call
  • Guaranteeing vervet monkey sightings on rainforest tours
  • Claiming this website sends or delivers contact-form messages

Editorial role

Tour pages describe typical patterns for cruise passengers. Operators set their own prices, inclusions and cut-off times. We are not those operators.

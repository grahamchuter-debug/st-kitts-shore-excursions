About this guide

Independent planning help for cruise passengers calling at Port Zante, Basseterre.

St Kitts Shore Excursion is a destination guide. We help you compare railway, Brimstone Hill, peninsula, beach, catamaran and private options against a realistic port day.

We do not operate tours, sell tickets, or take payment on this website. Bookings, if you make them, are with licensed operators you choose separately.

Ship call data is synced from the Caribbean Shore Excursions authority dataset for St Kitts only. Schedules can change. See methodology for how we treat that data.

Questions about planning? Use the contact enquiry page. The on-site form does not send email automatically.
